# 1.8.4 — 10 October 2026

A fix: Steam connects from the app again.

## Accounts

- **Steam connects in the app.** Since late September, connecting Steam in the app answered with a "missing_identity" error whatever link or name was typed. `/connect_steam` in the bot worked; the app did not. Now it does.

## For the super-admin

- **The user list shows everybody.** It used to list only people with at least one game account linked — a newcomer who had not connected Xbox, Steam or PSN yet was nowhere to be seen. Now they are listed with 🔘.

# 1.8.7 — hotfix — 23 October 2026

[Русский](1.8.7.ru.md) · **English**

A hotfix: lighter achievement icons, and a digest shows its game's cover.

## Posts

- **A digest shows the game's cover.** A digest of several achievements goes as one photo, since an album cannot carry the "Open Mini App" button. That photo used to be the first achievement's icon, as if it stood for all of them. It is now the game's cover; the icon only when there is none.

## App

- **Achievement icons load faster.** Xbox hands out achievement icons as full 1920×1080 pictures of 2–4 MB, and the app sent them to the phone whole. The bot now keeps them at most 1280 pixels on the longer side — tens of times lighter with no visible difference. Icons already downloaded were redone by the bot itself after the update.

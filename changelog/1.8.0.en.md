# 1.8.0 — 5 October 2026

It is no longer just a bot: the app is now called **Unlocked**, you can sign in with your email, install it on your phone, and it sends notifications of its own.

## Unlocked

- **A new name and logo.** The app is called Unlocked and has a new icon. The Telegram bot works as before.
- **Installs like an app.** In a browser on a phone or computer, Unlocked can be put on your screen: it opens without the browser, full screen. The app tells you how.
- **The phone's back button** steps back inside the app — closing a game, a sheet or a screen — instead of leaving it. In Telegram, its header Back button does the same.

## Signing in

- **Sign in by email.** Type your address, get a six-digit code, and you are in. You can sign in by email and by Telegram.
- **Sign-up by invite.** For now, somebody new signs up in a browser only with a code from a friend. Codes are made in Settings → Invite a friend: one code lets one person in, and it does not expire. You see who came by your code. Inside Telegram no code is needed.
- **Add your email.** If you have none yet, the app asks once — it is the main way in.
- **Settings → Profile → Sign-in.** Change your email, link Telegram (also through the bot, in one tap) or unlink it.
- **Merging accounts.** If two ways in made two profiles, the app offers to merge them and asks what to keep.

## Notifications

- **The app's own notifications.** A bell on Home: new followers, new friends, posts by people you follow, and an Xbox sign-in that stopped working. A tap opens the person, or the game straight away. Mark them all read at once.
- **Push.** To the phone or computer where you allowed it. Telegram messages from the bot are optional.
- **Friends' new posts.** You choose whose new achievements to hear about: friends only, everyone you follow, or nobody. About one game, at most once in three hours.

## Game news

- **"News" beside "Achievements" and "Ranking".** Everything the developers of the games you and your friends play posted on Steam that month: patches, announcements, sales — with pictures, as written, untranslated. A tap opens the post on Steam; tapping the game opens its page. The former "Feed" is now "Achievements".

## Settings

- **New settings.** Your profile on top, and inside it your accounts, sign-in and privacy. Every row says what is inside. "Publishing" is now "Chat posts".
- **Blocked people** are listed with your follows, and can be unblocked there.
- **One look for every form:** the same fields and buttons, a clear highlight while typing, green switches.

## Other

- **"Achievements"** everywhere in the Russian app and bot, instead of the slang word.
- **PSN trophies in the feed:** rarity first, then the trophy's tier — as everywhere else.
- **Old buttons in chats** open the game on the achiever's progress again, not yours.
- **Various interface improvements:** the header no longer jumps while loading, larger faces in notifications, grey arrows on every list.

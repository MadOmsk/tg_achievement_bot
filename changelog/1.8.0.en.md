# 1.8.0 — 7 October 2026

[Русский](1.8.0.ru.md) · **English**

It is no longer just a bot. The app is now called **Unlocked**: sign in with your email, install it on your phone, and get notifications, game news and video guides of its own.

## Unlocked

- **A new name and logo.** The app is called Unlocked and has a new icon. The Telegram bot works as before.
- **Installs like an app.** In a browser on your phone or computer, add Unlocked to your home screen — it opens full screen, without the browser. The app shows you how.
- **The phone's back button** steps back inside the app — closing a game, a page or a screen — instead of leaving it. In Telegram, the header's Back button does the same.
- **Comes back where you were.** A reload opens the same tab, game or profile.

## Signing in

- **Sign in by email.** Enter your address, get a six-digit code, and you're in. Email and Telegram both work.
- **Sign-up by invite.** A newcomer signs up in a browser with a code from a friend. Codes are in Settings → "Invite a friend": one code, one person, no expiry, and you see who came by yours. Inside Telegram no code is needed.
- **Settings → Profile → Sign-in.** Change your email, link Telegram (including in one tap through the bot) or unlink it.
- **Merging accounts.** Signed in two ways and ended up with two profiles? The app offers to merge them and asks what to keep.

## Notifications

- **A bell on Home.** New followers and friends, new achievements of the people you follow, news of your games. A feed by day: a face, who earned what, in which game and when. A tap opens the person or the game itself.
- **You choose what reaches your phone.** For push and for Telegram messages separately: whose posts (friends or everyone you follow), followers, friends, which game news — all, patches only or news only. By default everything stays in the bell and nothing is sent.
- **News of your games.** A new patch or post from the developer of a game you play arrives as a notification.
- **Friends are marked.** A friend's face carries a red handshake — in lists, search and notifications.

## Game news

- **A "News" tab** beside "Achievements" and "Ranking": everything the developers of the games you and your friends play posted on Steam this month — patches, announcements, sales, with pictures, and how many hours ago each came out. The old "Feed" is now "Achievements".
- **A page of its own for a post.** A post opens whole right in the app: the picture, the full text with its pictures, videos and links, and a link to Steam at the end.
- **"Updates" on a game's page** now holds both patches and news, with an All / News / Patches filter.

## Achievements and games

- **A page of its own for an achievement.** Its picture, rarity and points, when it was earned, its description — and "How to get it": the Steam community guides' tip in full.
- **Video guides.** An achievement's page shows TrophyTom's YouTube video, opening at the right minute. When the channel has a guide to the whole game, a video button appears in the game's header.
- **A platinum medal.** A game finished at 100% wears a platinum medal with an iridescent rim and the day of its last achievement. The first time you open your finished game, the medal bursts in with sparks.
- **A round progress.** A game in progress gets the same circle: its rim fills as you play, with the percentage and the day of the last achievement.
- **Completion times keep up.** A new game's HowLongToBeat times are read again while players are still timing it — no searching by hand.
- **A feed post takes its picture's shape.** A tall or square picture fills the post; under a wide one, the text sits below it.
- **Pictures full screen.** Any picture — in a post, a guide, the news, an avatar — opens full screen and zooms with two fingers or a double tap.

## Ranking

- **The day, the month and the year** in one table, with your share in percent. The leaders can be shown for the year too.
- **"Hunting together"** lists each achievement once, with the faces of everyone who earned it.

## Pages instead of drawers

- **No more drawers from the bottom.** Notifications, a person's profile, follows, the ranking's lists, an achievement from the feed and a game from search open as a page of their own, with a back arrow.
- **A person's profile** has their face, nickname and where they are in the header, the follow button on the right. A single platform starts open.
- **Following and followers** are one page: pick what to show at the header's right (following, friends, followers, blocked — with counts), and search by nickname under it.
- **A game from search** opens like a post: its picture, the completion times as a table, the description and a link to HowLongToBeat.
- **A calendar in the feed.** Tap a date to open the month's calendar, showing which days had something. The feed loads as you scroll, so a long month no longer slows it down.

## Settings

- **New settings.** Your profile on top, with accounts, sign-in and privacy inside. Each row says what is inside. "Publishing" is now "Posts in chats".
- **Blocked people** show in your follows list, where you can unblock them.
- **"Achievements"** everywhere in the app and the bot.

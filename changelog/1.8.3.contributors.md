# 1.8.3 — notes for contributors

## Version

- A patch release: `TRUNK_LINE` stays 8, no migration (D stays `089`), no new dependency. Mini App only.

## Mini App

- `components/game/achievement-page/AchievementLoader`: an achievement's page from a `FeedItem`, drawn at once from the item, then the game's row (`fetchGame` with the author's `person`) and the guides (`fetchGameGuides`). Opened through `useOpenAchievement()` (`shared/lib/game-open`), provided by `GameOpenProvider` next to the game page. The one-post sheet that `FeedList` and `ClubStats` opened (`UnlockCard` in a `Sheet`) is gone.
- No global month: `Club.tsx` keeps three — the Ranking's (`selectedMonth`, `pickMonth`), the Feed's (`feedMonth`, `pickFeedMonth`) and the live one. `components/person/games-section/GamesSection` is the Games block with its own month (`/club/people?month=` through a `load` callback) and the sort toggle; Home and `PersonProfile` both use it. `PersonProfile` takes `loadMonth` and `aside` (the follow control) in place of `monthChip`.
- `DayCalendar` takes `months` and `onMonth`: its arrows walk every month with posts and ask for the one stepped to; `FeedPosts` keys its day sections by place so a calendar left open survives the month landing under it.
- `.post.is-wide .post-copy` fills its slide (`flex: 1 0 auto`): slides share the tallest one's height.

## Bot

- `services/post_picture.py`: a post's picture laid out on a 1024 px square when it is sent (`Publisher._deliver` via `PublishJob.art`), originals untouched. Off until the admin picks a style (`post_picture_style`: `off` / `color` / `cover`, a registry row). The user card's `picture_test` action sends the person's latest achievement per platform in every style to the super-admin's DM (`handlers/delivery.send_picture_samples`, given to both panels as `AdminContext.send_picture_samples`).

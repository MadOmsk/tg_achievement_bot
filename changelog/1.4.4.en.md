# 1.4.4 — 25 September 2026

More precise platforms, a fuller achievement history, and Xbox 360 working again.

## Notifications

- **The version of the game you played.** An achievement card and a digest now name the version of the game the achievement was earned in: an Xbox 360 game stays `XBOX 360` even on a Series X, a One game is `XBOX One`, Smart Delivery is the version of the console you played on, Play Anywhere on PC is `XBOX PC`, the cloud gets a ☁. PlayStation the same: a PS4 game on a PS5 is `PlayStation 4`. When the device is unknown the bot does not guess and says just `XBOX` or `PSN`.
- **Short names** in `/recent` (`Series X|S`, `One`, `360`, `PS5`), a game's release platforms in the games lists (`XPA`, `One | Series`, `PS4 | PS5`), and the player's device in `/online`.
- **Rarity is just a percentage**, without the word.
- **Achievement descriptions in a digest** are in italics.

## Xbox

- **Xbox 360 achievements arrive again.** Since 1.4.0 the bot stopped seeing new Xbox 360 achievements — fixed; the missed ones come in the next time the game is played.
- **Real Xbox 360 achievement icons** instead of the game's cover.
- **The whole history on connect**: the bot reads the full game history, not the latest 200 — for long-time players that is hundreds of Xbox 360 games.

## PlayStation

- **DLC trophies in older games.** DLC trophies in games loaded before add-ons were supported are now fetched, and the group counter (“Main Game · 3/51”) is right.
- Trophies of games that once failed to save are fetched again.

## Steam

- **Free-to-play games** — Destiny 2, Apex Legends and others — now count in the history.
- **Half-Life 2's episodes** are visible again: Valve folded them into Half-Life 2 and they dropped out of the library. The 👾 completed-games count now matches Steam's.
- **Covers for older games** in the Mini App.

## Other

- An achievement's names, descriptions and rarity live in one place — the chat and the Mini App show the same.
- A deleted chat is switched off automatically instead of receiving the bot's messages forever.
- Less load on Xbox: apps and games without achievements are not polled.

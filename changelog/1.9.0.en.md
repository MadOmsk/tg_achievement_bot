# 1.9.0 — 27 October 2026

[Русский](1.9.0.ru.md) · **English**

The Mini App has a new design, points of its own and a season pass.

- **A new look for the whole app**: light and dark.
- **Points of our own instead of G**, the same on every platform: a common achievement is 10, rare 20, legendary 30, mythic 50. The ranking and the month count them.
- **A season pass**: collect points over the month and claim new app themes. Every month is a new season; the themes you claim stay yours.
- **The feed in cards**: the achievement's picture large, its rarity, who got it with you.
- **One rarity scale on every platform**: rare, legendary, mythic.
- **A game on several platforms**: progress on each one apart, with a switch on the game page.
- **The month on Home and on a profile can be turned**, and Home shows your place among friends.
- **Your favorite games' news right in the feed.**
- **Avatars**: with no Telegram photo, your game account's picture is shown.

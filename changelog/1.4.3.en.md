# 1.4.3 — 24 September 2026

A small update on top of 1.4: a “Close” button on the bot’s replies, a completion badge per platform, the right Xbox gamertag, a safer `/delete_last`, summaries that no longer pile up, and sync fixes.

## Chat

- **A “Close” button.** The bot’s replies in a group — `/stats`, `/summary_day`, `/summary_month`, `/recent`, `/online`, `/help`, the date calendars — now carry a “Close” button: one tap removes the message from the chat. Closing `/online` also stops its auto-refresh.
- **`/delete_last` leaves achievements alone** (#101). The command deletes the bot’s latest message of any kind — a prompt, help, stats — but never an achievement or trophy notification, single or grouped. The admin panel’s and the Mini App’s own “delete last” follow the same rule.
- **Summaries no longer pile up.** `/summary_day` and `/summary_month` remove their previous copy on a new call again — the bot used to fail to remember the summary it had sent, so old ones stayed in the chat.

## Badges and names

- **A completion badge per platform.** A 100%-completed game on any platform and a PSN platinum used to share one badge. Now each platform has its own: 🌀 Xbox, 👾 Steam, 💠 PSN platinum — in `/stats`, the personal panel, the admin user card and the Mini App.
- **Your current Xbox gamertag.** The Xbox line in `/stats` and the panel shows the modern gamertag, falling back to the classic one. An outdated name could linger there before.

## Sync

- **Xbox 360 games.** Xbox 360 titles, including ones played through backward compatibility, are recognised as 360 games more reliably, and their achievements are moved to the right platform.
- **A steadier database.** The database now waits for a lock to clear instead of failing straight away when several jobs write at once.

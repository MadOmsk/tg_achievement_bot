## Backend

- `repo.account_facts(tg_id, account_platform)` (`db/repo/_stats.py`): games, rare (by the global threshold), newest dated unlock — added with `month_count` and `linked_at` to each platform of `build_person_payload`.
- `repo.following_of_person` / `followers_of_person` and `GET /api/mini/people/{id}/following|followers`: another person's lists, relation relative to the viewer, only when `can_view_activity` allows; rows carry `is_me`.

# 1.2.0 — 18 September 2026

One thing this time: the bot now has a **Mini App** inside Telegram. The
slash commands are untouched and work exactly as before — the app is a second
door, not a replacement.

## What's new

**The Mini App.** Open your chat with the bot and tap the menu button to the
left of the message field. There are four screens inside.

**Profile.** Your platforms in one list: achievements and trophies,
gamerscore, PlayStation level, platinums and games finished at 100%. The
settings live here too — language, timezone, whether profile links are shown,
and your per-chat subscriptions with their rarity threshold and digest size.

**Club.** The chat's feed: who unlocked what and when, with game and
achievement art, progress through the game and the trophy's own tier. It
pages by month. Beside it: who is online right now and what they are playing,
and the month's totals with its players and games.

**A person's card.** Tap a name in the feed or the roster and you get
everything the bot knows about them: platforms, counters, their own feed.

**HowLongToBeat.** Search a game and get the completion-time card — the same
thing `/hltb` does, without typing a command.

**Super-admins** also get the admin panel: platform keys, limits, the list of
people and chats, and each chat's own settings.

## What not to expect

The app reads **only what the bot has already stored**. It never calls Xbox,
Steam or PlayStation for fresh data — the same rule `/stats` and `/summary`
follow. So it opens instantly, but it shows the picture as of the last poll
rather than this exact second.

## What is broken right now

Said plainly, so you don't think it's you:

- **You cannot connect an account from the app** — the nickname field does
  not accept text. Use `/connect_xbox`, `/connect_steam`, `/connect_psn`;
  those work.
- **Disconnecting PlayStation shows an error** even though it works: tap a
  second time and the account is unlinked.
- **Toggles don't show when they are on** — they look the same grey either
  way. The value itself is saved correctly.

All three are being fixed.

## For the detail-minded

The version in `/help` now starts with `1.2`. The app is served from `/app/`
on the same domain as the bot, and only works from inside Telegram — opened
anywhere else it will politely tell you there is nothing to do there.

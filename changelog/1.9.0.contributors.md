- The Mini App is design E, "Expressive": its look is `docs/design-system.md`,
  its one source `webapp/src/styles/tokens.css` and `webapp/src/ui/`.

  reads Xbox's `mythic` mark (`RecentAchievement.mythic`), no migration.

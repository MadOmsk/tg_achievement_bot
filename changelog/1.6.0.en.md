# 1.6.0 — 28 September 2026

The Mini App's game page now figures itself out — HowLongToBeat completion times and descriptions, no searching required — and gains smooth tabs. Consistent frosted sticky headers, game sorting, section counters, and redesigned online status marks.

## Mini App

- **Completion times and game descriptions.** The game page gains an "About" tab: hours for the main story, extras, and full completionist runs, plus HowLongToBeat's own description. Matches happen automatically in the background, once, forever — no user search or manual confirmation required. Loaded independently so achievements open instantly without waiting.
- **Tabs on the game page.** "Achievements" and "About" switch by tap or swipe, like a native gallery.
- **Game sorting.** The "Games" section on Home gains a sort toggle: by recent activity (default) or by achievement count for the selected month.
- **Section counters.** "Friends" and "Games" headers on Home now show their exact count next to the title.
- **Consistent sticky headers.** Feed, Stats, and Profile pages now share a unified blurred header (`backdrop-filter`). The month picker chip moves into the header itself, keeping content space tidy.
- **Polished online indicators.** Avatar presence badges drop heavy solid circles in favor of crisp, green-tinted platform marks (Xbox, Steam, PlayStation) at a consistent size.
- **Recent achievement cards.** Game titles in minimal cards sit neatly above the achievement name as a subtle tag, with the description shown beneath.
- **Month switch skeletons.** Switching months on Home and Stats displays skeleton placeholders instead of layout jumps or stale data.
- **Smooth profile transitions.** Opening another player's profile resets scroll before paint, eliminating scroll flashes.

## Bot and Chats

- **Mini App button in notifications & digests.** Single achievement notifications and digests now include an inline "Open Mini App" button. Because Telegram albums (media groups) do not support inline keyboards, digests with a Mini App button are delivered as a single photo card with the primary achievement icon and the complete digest text, ensuring the button is rendered directly under the message.
- **Pinned promo message for chats.** New `/promo` command (and direct send from `/admin` chat card) posts a concise gaming club intro with a Mini App button, tailored for Telegram pinned message bars.

## Bot & HLTB Service

- **Automatic title matcher.** A dedicated pure scoring service (`hltb_match`) with two-tier name normalization, Roman numeral conversion, edition/platform tail stripping, strict number preservation, and Steam AppID verification.
- **Database indexing.** Migration 063 adds an index on `seen_achievements(title_id)`, keeping first-played year lookups fast on large libraries.
- **Comprehensive test suite.** Complete unit test coverage for the HLTB matcher engine.

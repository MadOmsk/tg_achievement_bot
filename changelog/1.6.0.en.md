# 1.6.0 — 28 September 2026

The Mini App's game page now figures itself out — HowLongToBeat completion times and a description, no searching required — and gets a proper tab bar.

## Mini App

- **Completion times and game description.** The game page has a new "About" tab: hours for the main story, a full completionist run, and everything in between, plus HowLongToBeat's own description. Matched automatically, once, forever — nothing to search or confirm.
- **Tabs on the game page.** "Achievements" and "About" switch by tap or by swipe, like a gallery.
- Various Mini App interface polish: smoother loading and tidier screens while data comes in.

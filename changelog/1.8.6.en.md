# 1.8.6 — hotfix — 17 October 2026

[Русский](1.8.6.ru.md) · **English**

A hotfix: pictures of new Steam games.

## Posts

- **New Steam games have their achievement picture again.** Steam moved achievement icons to a new server, and the old one no longer has those of recent games. Posts about such games went out as text with no picture. The bot now asks for the icon where it is now.

## App

- **Covers for new Steam games.** Recent Steam games keep their covers at new addresses the bot did not find, so the app showed no picture for them. The bot now asks the Steam store where the cover is. Games without one get it by themselves after the update.

# 1.5.1 — 26 September 2026

More Mini App: a profile now shows games and a gallery of the latest achievements, the game page looks better, and settings can delete your account.

## Mini App

- **A profile is its games.** Under the gallery, the month's games replace the list of achievements — the one with the newest achievement first. Each shows the cover, overall progress as a bar (light blue for a finished game), how many were earned and when the last one came. Tap a game to open its page with that person's progress. The stretch earned this month is brighter on the bar.
- **A gallery of the latest achievements.** On the home page and in a profile: up to five latest achievements across the full width of the picture, with the achievement's name and its game. Tap the game to open its page.
- **The game page.**
  - The game's picture across the top with a plate under it — percentage, bar and the count. A 100%-completed game gets a big platinum medal with a glow and sparkles instead, and a cool filter on the picture.
  - Secret achievements follow the "show secrets" setting, in the list and in the opened card (a tap reveals one).
  - It always opens on what was earned; the lock switches to what is still to earn, and the two-rings icon turns on the comparison with that person.
  - Whose achievements you see is written in the bar under the game's name, along with how much was earned (G, achievements or trophies).
  - An achievement's description is no longer cut off and can take several lines. An achievement without a picture of its own shows the game's cover.
- **PSN trophy medals.** Bronze, silver, gold and platinum are drawn as the same medals everywhere, and the game's bar shows only the tiers you have.
- **Tap a game.** The game name and progress block on big cards, and the games on the stats screen, open the game's page.
- **No empty lists on a game page.** If none of a game's achievements is earned, or all are, there is no lock — the list that has achievements is shown at once.
- **Profile.** Under the name in the bar: a status ("Online", "Offline" or what they are playing). You are no longer in your own friends list.
- **Delete account** — a trash icon next to the "Accounts" heading in settings, with Telegram's own confirmation. The language and time zone section is now called "General".
- **Font and readability.** The app is set in Inter, small text is bigger, and achievement names and rarity sit on one line. Achievements not earned yet are no longer washed out: only the icon is dimmed.
- **Feed.** Opens at once, the posts build in the background; the feed under a game page is no longer painted, which removed the artifacts. The "+DLC" badge has a new look and days are evenly spaced.

# 1.6.3 — 2 October 2026

Achievement tips from Steam guides and an updates tab for each game in the Mini App, one rarity threshold for every chat, and help with hidden achievements.

## Mini App

- **Achievement tips.** An achievement with a book in its corner unfolds into a tip on how to get it, from the Steam community's most popular guides, with links, pictures, videos and tables. A secret one is revealed first, as before.
- **"Updates" tab.** A game's latest patches from its developer's announcements on Steam. Xbox and PlayStation games show their Steam version's patches; the tab is hidden when there are none.
- **Various interface improvements.** Tidier game tabs, empty sections and progress bars.

## Bot

- **One rarity threshold for every chat.** What counts as rare (💎, "Rare" mode, the rare counts in `/stats` and the summaries) is now decided by one threshold — 10%. The administrator changes it in `/admin` → global settings; a chat card no longer has one.
- **When achievements are hidden.** A Steam or PSN screen whose achievements are hidden by privacy settings explains that the bot can't see new ones, and puts "🔓 How to open achievements" first — the steps, a link to the settings and "🔄 Check again".
- **Sturdier restarts.** If Telegram is unreachable for a minute at start-up, the bot waits and tries again instead of hanging.

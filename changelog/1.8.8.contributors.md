## Operations

- `scripts/xbox-deploy.sh`: after its backup, the deploy prunes its own copies (`<db>-YYYYMMDD-HHMMSS.db`) past the newest `KEEP_BACKUPS` — 8 on prod, 5 on test. A copy named by hand stays. 115 kept copies had filled the 24 GB disk to 96%.
- `.github/workflows/ci.yml`: `scp`/`ssh` connect on port 22022 with `-o HostKeyAlias="$HOST"`. The host key is the same on both ports, so the pinned `DEPLOY_KNOWN_HOSTS` entry matches and the secret is unchanged. Once a deploy over 22022 has succeeded, port 22 can be closed on the VPS (`Port 22` in `/etc/ssh/sshd_config.d/00-custom.conf` and the `ssh.socket` override).
- The VPS's SSH has refused passwords since 2026-10-23 (root by key only); journald is capped at 300 MB (`/etc/systemd/journald.conf.d/size.conf`).

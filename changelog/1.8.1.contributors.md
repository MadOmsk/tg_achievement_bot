# 1.8.1 — notes for contributors

## Version

- A patch release: `TRUNK_LINE` stays 8, no tag of its own. No migrations; D stays `088`.

## Backend

- `/api/mini/auth/config` answers `bot_id` (the public part of the bot token) for Telegram's own sign-in page.
- `/api/mini/auth/email/start` checks a new address's invite before any mail (`invite_required` / `invite_invalid`); the answer now tells a known address from an unknown one — the owner's choice. Tests: `test_a_new_address_is_asked_for_its_invite_before_any_mail`, `test_somebody_known_signs_in_with_no_invite`.

- **Chain 1 is now the nickname, else `id<person id>`** (owner, 2026-10-07): `naming.person_name(person_id=, handle=)`, no username, platform nicknames or Telegram id. `link_platform_account` gives a nickname to somebody who has none. The admin's user card, in the bot and the Mini App, lists every way in (`services/logins.py`). `scripts/render_screen.py admin-user-card` passes a person id.
- A post, a digest and `/online` name the person by chain 1 (`Publisher._person_label`, `views/online._row_name`); the platform's own nickname stays only where a line is about one account. `User.handle` is now the shown form with its digits (`_accounts._USER_COLUMNS` reads `HANDLE_SHOWN` as `handle_shown`) — `/stats`, the admin's card and posts had lost the `#1234`.

## Mini App

- `shared/lib/home-cache`: the viewer and Home's four answers kept in `localStorage` per Telegram account (or the browser), shown at once and replaced by the answers; cleared on sign-out and account deletion. `App.reload(true)` opens on the cached `/me`; the browser no longer asks `/me` twice.
- The sign-in screen goes to `oauth.telegram.org/auth` in the same tab and finishes on `#tgAuthResult=`; the Login Widget stays where no `bot_id` is known and in Settings → «Вход».
- Opening a person from a follows list or a card happens in the same turn as the list closes (no `requestAnimationFrame`): a frame later the profile's history entry was pushed without the tap, and a phone's back skipped it.
- Skeletons put bars inside the real elements' text lines (`.skel-inline`) or in boxes measured against them: the game page (`RowsSkel lines={2}`, the medal's date), notifications, the person card's open account, the ranking's table and sections.
- The dev server exposes `window.__backStack()` (layers open, history entries held) for checking the back button; a build leaves it out.
- Icons: `logo.svg` (rounded tile), `logo-192/512.png`, `apple-touch-icon.png` and `logo-maskable-512.png` (full-bleed squares), `badge-96.png` (the push badge, one colour).

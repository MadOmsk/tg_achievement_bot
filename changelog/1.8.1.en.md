# 1.8.1 — 7 October 2026

Opens faster, signs in more easily, and has a new icon.

## Unlocked

- **A new icon.** A platinum "U" — an open shackle with a glowing tip, in the app's own colours. Your phone may keep the old one until the app is installed again.
- **Home opens at once.** The phone remembers Home from last time and shows it straight away; fresh data slips in by itself.
- **Loading looks like the page.** While notifications, a game, a person's card or the ranking load, their placeholders sit exactly where the content will.

## Signing in

- **Telegram sign-in on phones.** "Sign in with Telegram" confirms in the same tab and comes back to the app — it used to land on the sign-in screen again.
- **"Sign in" instead of "Get code".**
- **The invite before the email.** Somebody new is asked for an invite first, and only then gets the email. Anybody the app already knows signs in without one, as before.

## Small things

- **The back button** no longer leaves the app after opening a person from a follows list.
- **"Mark all read"** is now a bell with a tick.
- **Every video** in guides opens with the same arrow.

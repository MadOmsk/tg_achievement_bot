# 0.3.0 — November 2026

[Русский](0.3.0.ru.md) · **English**

The app moved to a new database, games are translated as you open them, the season pass tells you when a season starts, and time zones now go by city.

And a new numbering: until 1.0 the app lives in 0.x versions. The old 2.2.1 now reads 0.2.1, so the next one is 0.3.0.

## The app

- **Games are translated when opened.** If a game's description or achievements are not in Russian yet, the text shows blurred and turns Russian by itself within seconds. No need to leave the page.
- **Game news in Russian**, and the guides' tips too.
- **Time zones by city.** Summer time follows by itself. The list is laid out as in Windows, and your device's zone is one tap away.
- **DLC and edition pages**, a reworked gallery and search on the game page.
- **Home.** The month card stands on the season's cover, in its colours and with its name, and the door to the season pass fills with a wave towards the next level.

## Season pass

- **Season start.** At 12:00 on the 1st, your time, you get a notice if you have not opened the pass yet. Switched off in Settings: "Season start".
- **A season has a cover.** The pass's page takes its colours from it.

## PlayStation

- **Your own PSN token.** You can add your account's token: it is read more often and does not depend on the shared token. Settings → Accounts → PlayStation, or the button in the bot. Optional: without one everything works as before, polled less often.

## Chats and roles

- **Four roles:** user, chat admin, app admin and super-admin. Whoever adds the bot to a group is its admin, and can hand the role to another member of the chat.
- **The publishing switch** of a chat now belongs to its admin.

## Sign-in

- **A new sign-in screen**, mail comes from Unlocked, and when a passkey fails the app sends the email code by itself.

## Under the hood

- **A new database, PostgreSQL.** Nothing changes for you; the app handles more at once and keeps its data more safely.

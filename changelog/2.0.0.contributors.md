# 2.0.0 — notes for contributors

- The games catalog (#147): versions, games, DLC, editions, series, HLTB
  and store pages in the main database, migrations **090–131**. Store
  collection (the octopus) waits for the super-admin's switch, off by
  default. A catalog is carried between databases with
  `scripts/catalog_transfer.py`; the operator's tools are
  `scripts/catalog_viewer.py` and the review lists.
- The Mini App is design E, "Expressive": its look is `docs/design-system.md`,
  its one source `webapp/src/styles/tokens.css` and `webapp/src/ui/`.
- The feed's card is `webapp/src/parts/MagazinePost.tsx`, shared with a
  person's page; the old `Post` / `NewsPost` are gone.
- Rarity is one scale for every platform (`app/views.ts::rareLevel`); the feed
  reads Xbox's `mythic` mark (`RecentAchievement.mythic`).
- The app's own points replace G in the Mini App: `_sql.points` on the server
  (`ChatMemberStat.points`, `repo.points_for_person`), `views.POINTS` in the
  app — keep the two scales the same. The bot's chat messages keep G.
- The game page's `progress` and players carry `lists` (one per platform list,
  never summed); `repo.game_players` returns them.
- The season pass: `bot/services/season_pass.py` (the levels), `web/mini_pass.py`
  (`GET /api/mini/pass`, `POST /api/mini/pass/claim`, which checks the points),
  migration **131** `pass_claims`; a merge of two people moves the claims.
  The palettes drop to six (Lilac, Forest, Garnet are gone; a device on one
  falls back to Violet).
- Faces (`/api/mini/avatar/p/…`): the files on disk first, then Telegram by
  `file_id`, then the game accounts' pictures (`repo.face_paths`).
- A new major: `MAJOR = 2`, `TRUNK_LINE = 0` (`bot/version.py`); production reads `2.0.…`, working branches `2.1.…`. Tagged `v2.0.0`.

# 2.2.0 — 28 October 2026

[Русский](2.2.0.ru.md) · **English**

One games database for prod and the test server, game descriptions in Russian, Xbox 360 add-ons and posters without their band.

## The app

- **Game descriptions in Russian.** When a store has no Russian description, the app translates the English one once, the first time the game is opened in Russian; from then on it reads in Russian. English text a store hands back as Russian no longer passes for a translation.
- **Xbox 360 games show their add-ons** sold on their own — Gears of War 3's Season Pass, for one.
- **Xbox 360 posters without the "XBOX 360" band** — now those taken from the game's Xbox store page too.

## The games catalog

- **One games database for prod and the test server.** What the octopus assembles shows on both at once; the catalog is no longer carried from one server to the other.

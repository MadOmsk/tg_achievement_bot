# 1.8.9 — hotfix — 25 October 2026

[Русский](1.8.9.ru.md) · **English**

A hotfix: Steam achievements are caught up again, even when the bot cannot see you in a game.

## What is fixed

- **Steam achievements earned while Invisible or offline now reach the chat.** Once an hour the bot re-reads the games you played in the last two weeks and publishes what it missed. That check found nothing: Steam does not say when a game was last launched, so the bot took every game for one long abandoned. Now such games are always re-read. Achievements from the last day go to the chat; older ones are kept in the statistics without a post.

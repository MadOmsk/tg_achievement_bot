# 2.1.0 — 28 October 2026

[Русский](2.1.0.ru.md) · **English**

A season pass with prizes, game art from our own server, faces inside Telegram, and Xbox 360 covers without their frame.

## The season pass

- **A pass of its own every month.** The month's points climb its levels; the levels hold the app's themes and prizes: games from our catalog and codes for anything, with the platform they are for. A prize gives one code to a person until its codes run out; how many are left, and who took them, is shown.
- **Levels tell you.** When your points reach a new level a notice comes, and one for new prizes too. Both can be switched off in the notification settings.
- **Earlier months can be leafed through**: your points, what you got, your codes.
- **A month may have a name and a theme of its own** — Halloween brought a pumpkin palette.

## The app

- **Faces show inside Telegram again.** In the app opened from Telegram people showed as initials. Faces load now, and on a person's page their face also lights the header.
- **Game posters, covers and banners come from our server.** The server downloads a picture once, shrinks it and serves it itself — faster, and independent of the stores.
- **Xbox 360 covers without their "XBOX 360" frame** (nor its kinds: Platinum Hits, Games for Windows LIVE, ARCADE). The same in chat posts.
- **An Xbox game's achievements are split into the main game and its add-ons** where part of the list came later.
- **The favorite heart** sits in the game page's top bar, with its count.
- **The month's games come newest first** on Home, a profile, the Ranking and Search.

## The games catalog

- **The catalog the octopus assembled is now on prod**: more games, versions, DLC, editions and HowLongToBeat entries.
- **The catalog's labels and weights come with every release** from git; when they have not changed, nothing happens.

# 1.5.1 — notes for contributors

## Dependencies

- **New: `@fontsource-variable/inter`** — Inter is self-hosted (Cyrillic, Latin, Latin-ext subsets, `styles/fonts.css`).
- `swiper` stays (the creative effect); `embla-carousel-react` was tried and removed.

## Mini App

- The look of every progress bar lives in one file, `styles/progress.css` (`--bar-h` sets thickness, `--fill-*` the colours); the old per-card overrides were removed. Loading placeholders are `RowsSkel` / `PageSkel` in `shared/lib/skeleton`.

## Server

- The test bot no longer deactivates chats it cannot reach: `Publisher._send` skips them until the next start and the daily summary counts the day as done, when `is_test()` (as `release_notify` already did). Production behaviour is unchanged.
- `TitleCatalogService` calls `PsnAuth.get_client()` and `Repo.get_platform_link()` (both names were wrong).
- No migrations — D stays `062`.

# 1.4.0 — 23 September 2026

Major update 1.4: full game achievement catalog with details view in the Mini App, local achievement icon caching, full Xbox 360 support with rarity and icons, redesigned group `/panel` hub, focused `/help` command list, curated group slash menu, interactive date navigation for stats, and deep poller optimizations.

## Achievement Catalog & Mini App (#99, #80)

- **Complete game achievement catalog.** The bot now tracks the entire roster of achievements for supported titles rather than only achievements unlocked by registered players.
- **Game details in Mini App.** Selecting a game displays full progress: unlocked vs. locked achievements, descriptions, rarity badges, and Gamerscore / trophy tiers.
- **Local icon storage & caching.** Achievement icons are downloaded and stored on disk organized by platform and game (`/data/achievement_icons/`), ensuring fast loading and eliminating dependence on external CDNs.
- **24-hour freshness TTL.** Game catalog data is cached with a 24-hour expiration window, avoiding unnecessary upstream API queries.

## Telegram Group Navigation & Menus (#19, #75, #54)

- **Group `/panel` command.** Introduced `/panel` in group chats to display a compact bot card, subscriber roster, and interactive quick-access hub buttons (Mini App, stats, HLTB, recent games). In direct messages, `/panel` continues to open personal account settings.
- **Streamlined `/help`.** Removed redundant buttons and narrative text from `/help`; it now presents a clean, readable listing of all supported commands.
- **Curated group slash menu.** Typing `/` in group chats now shows only 4 primary commands: `/panel` (menu and hub buttons), `/subscribe`, `/hltb`, and `/help`.
- **Interactive date navigation & calendar.** Statistics and summary commands (`/stats`, `/who`, `/summary_day`, `/summary_month`) now feature inline day/month stepping buttons and an interactive calendar date picker.
- **One-click publication cycling.** The publication button on the chat hub now cycles between modes in place: *All achievements* → *Rare only* → *Disabled*.

## Platforms & Xbox 360

- **Full Xbox 360 support.** Native icon retrieval, automated rarity calculation, and backfill handling for classic Xbox 360 titles.
- **Backward compatibility awareness.** The bot identifies Xbox 360 titles running under backward compatibility on Xbox One and Xbox Series X|S, formatting device codenames appropriately in announcements.
- **Precise platform labels.** Clear platform badges in listings and notifications: *Xbox Series X|S*, *Xbox One*, *Xbox 360*, and *PC*.
- **Accurate Xbox completed games.** Xbox 100% completions now calculate accurately against the full official title catalog.

## Poller & Synchronization (#89, #90, #78)

- **Unified multi-platform sync.** In the personal panel, refresh and sync are unified into a single action that polls all linked platforms (Xbox, Steam, PSN) concurrently.
- **Instant dormant wakeup.** Triggering sync immediately awakens players from dormant status.
- **Steam catch-up.** When the bot starts, achievements earned during downtime are backfilled without flooding subscriber chats.
- **Tiered polling.** Active players are polled every 60s, while players inactive for >14 days enter dormant status to preserve API quota.
- **PSN throttling & exit poll.** Calibrated request delays for PlayStation Network with exit polling on title close.

## Security & Account Management

- **Account deletion with double confirmation.** Users, admins, and the Mini App can cleanly unlink platforms and purge accounts with double confirmation.
- **Data cleanup & cooldowns.** Unlinking a platform purges local cached achievement rows and enforces an anti-abuse reconnect cooldown.
- **Admin privacy indicators.** Administrative listings show Steam and PSN profile visibility status.

## Infrastructure

- Deployment script `scripts/xbox-deploy.sh` auto-synchronizes to `/usr/local/bin/xbox-deploy` on server deploy.
- Fixed SIGPIPE in deployment validation under bash `pipefail`.

# 1.4.5 — 25 September 2026

A big Mini App update: a game page, jumping to a day, and new carousels.

## Mini App

- **Game page.** Tap the game block on a big achievement card, in a profile gallery or in the games list on the stats screen to open the game's page: its cover edge to edge and every achievement it has. A lock switches between earned and not yet earned. Somebody else's card opens on what they have earned, and **"Compare"** puts both of you in one list.
- **Jump to a day.** Day labels ("Today", dates) in a profile and in the feed open a month calendar and scroll to the day you pick.
- **New carousels** in the feed and profile galleries: smoother, and they loop.
- **A lighter feed**: posts load as you scroll, and pictures appear whole instead of line by line.
- **Stats**: the month's games strip and the headline tiles are back, with new **finds** (achievements rarer than 0.5%) and **hunts** lists that open details on a tap.
- **Secret achievements** show their score, rarity and game — only the name and description stay hidden.
- The avatar opens full screen; settings and the admin screens are plain lists.

## Other

- The test bot no longer switches off chats it is not a member of.
- Refreshing a PSN game's trophy list in the Mini App works again.

# 2.2.1 — hotfix — 28 October 2026

[Русский](2.2.1.ru.md) · **English**

Hotfix: saves no longer fail while the games collector runs.

- **Saves are reliable again.** While the games catalog's collector took its next game from the queue, the bot's other jobs (refreshing covers, for one) sometimes could not save and put their work off to the next time.
- **Xbox 360 add-ons are back.** Some of the add-ons found were lost when prod's and the test server's games databases were joined; they were found again.

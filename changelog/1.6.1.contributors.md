# 1.6.1 — notes for contributors

## Promo message persistence

- `bot/handlers/chat.py` & `bot/handlers/admin.py`: `/promo` in chats and "📢 Отправить промо в чат" in admin panel wrap `bot.send_message`/`message.answer` in `stats_category()`. Messages logged with `is_system = 0` are excluded from `due_system_messages()` in `bot/poller/message_cleanup.py` and are never auto-deleted.
- `tests/test_mini_app_entry.py`: Added assertions verifying `_stats_category` context is active during `/promo` and admin chat promo dispatch.

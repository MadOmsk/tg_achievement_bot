# 1.7.0 — 2 October 2026

People and follows: everyone has a nickname of their own, you can follow friends, the feed shows the people you follow, and the app opens in a browser too.

## Mini App

- **A nickname instead of a name.** Everyone is shown by their nickname — in the app, the feed and the bot's messages; real names are no longer shown anywhere. On the first visit the app offers to keep your nickname or pick another. It can change once every 30 days.
- **Follows, as on Xbox.** Follow anybody, no approval needed; following each other makes you friends. Following and followers live in the Friends block on Home.
- **A feed of your friends.** The feed, the ranking and the people on Home are now about you and the people you follow, not about one chat.
- **A Search tab.** Finds people by nickname and games. People from your chats are suggested there too.
- **A person's card.** Tap an author in the feed or a nickname on a profile: accounts, this month's games, follow and block.
- **Privacy.** Choose who sees your activity: everyone, friends only, or nobody. Your nickname and avatar are always visible.
- **Sign in from a browser.** The app also opens outside Telegram, with Telegram sign-in.
- **New settings.** Settings and the admin panel are grouped clearly, and Publishing answers three questions: what, where and from which accounts.
- **Various interface improvements.** New dropdowns, one look for buttons, tidier cards and loading.

## Bot

- **New follower message.** The bot tells you in a DM when someone follows you or you become friends.
- **Choosing a Microsoft account.** Connecting Xbox now asks which Microsoft account to use instead of silently taking the last one.

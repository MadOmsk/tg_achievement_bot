# 2.0.1 — hotfix — 28 October 2026

[Русский](2.0.1.ru.md) · **English**

A hotfix: faces inside Telegram, game art from our own server, Xbox 360 covers without their frame.

## The app

- **Faces show inside Telegram again.** In the app opened from Telegram people showed as initials: their pictures were asked for without Telegram's signature, and the server refused them. Faces load now, and open full screen from a person's page.
- **Game posters, covers and banners come from our server.** The app used to load them straight from the Xbox, Steam and PlayStation stores. Now the server downloads a picture once, shrinks it to a sensible size and serves it itself: faster, and independent of other people's servers.
- **Xbox 360 covers without their frame.** A portrait cover loses its "XBOX 360" header (and its kinds: Platinum Hits, Games for Windows LIVE, ARCADE), a square one the green bar on its left. The same in chat posts.

## For the super-admin

- **The games catalog the octopus assembled on the test server is carried to prod** by one command on the server (`xbox-deploy catalog`). The database is backed up before the replacement and the bot stops for about a minute; the dry run checks it all on a copy of the database and leaves prod alone. The labels stay those in git; prod keeps its own octopus queue.
- **The catalog's labels and weights come with every release** from `catalog/` in git; when they have not changed, nothing happens.

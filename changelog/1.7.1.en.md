# 1.7.1 — 4 October 2026

A new feed, person cards with details per account, a heart on your friends and "People you may know" suggestions.

## Mini App

- **A new feed.** Each post is the achievement's picture, large: the author and time on top, and on frosted glass at the bottom its name, points, rarity, description and game. Several achievements in one game swipe round in a loop. Tapping a post opens the game on that person's progress; tapping the author opens their profile.
- **Person cards.** Each account shows one number — Xbox points, PlayStation trophies or Steam achievements; tap it for a table: games, completed 100%, rare, this month, latest achievement, PSN level and medals. Tapping yourself on Home opens your own card.
- **Other people's follows.** A person's profile shows whom they follow, and their card opens their followers and following as lists — with a Friends tab.
- **A heart on friends.** Friends carry a heart on their avatar and come first in lists.
- **"People you may know".** The Search tab suggests friends of friends: people followed by those you follow.
- **Clearer follow menu.** The button's menu lists actions: Follow, Add as friend, Unfollow, Remove from friends, Block.
- **Quieter nickname digits.** The digits after a taken nickname (#4821) are muted, and shown right in the field on the nickname page.
- **Games on a profile.** This month's gain sits at the right under the bar; a finished game glows platinum.
- **Connecting Steam and PSN.** New forms, with a note on what to make public in privacy settings.
- **Various interface improvements.** A tidier Home gallery, spacing, loading skeletons and the settings icon.

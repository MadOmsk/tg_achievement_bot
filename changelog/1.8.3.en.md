# 1.8.3 — 9 October 2026

An achievement opens on its own, and the month is picked where it matters.

## Achievements

- **Tapping a post opens the achievement.** In the feed, Home's gallery and the ranking's lists ("Hunting together", rare finds), a tap opens the achievement's own page: its picture, description, when it was earned, and how to get it — the guides' tip and videos, when there are any. The game opens from its name.
- **The shade under a post's text** now reaches the very bottom, on slides with a short description too.

## Months

- **No month picker in the page header.** Home and a profile show this month, and the gallery on top is always about it.
- **The games' month lives in the Games block.** Pick it at the right of the block's header, the sort next to it; only the list of games changes.
- **The feed changes month in its calendar.** The arrows in the calendar under a day's date switch the month, and a day can be picked right away.
- News is always this month's; the ranking still picks its month in its header.

## Profile

- **Follow from a profile's header.** At the right: "Follow", or "Following ⌄" to unfollow or block.
- As much room between following and games as between the gallery and following.

# 1.4.3 — notes for contributors

## Version

- **No trunk line bump.** `TRUNK_LINE` stays `4` and no tag is cut (owner's call): `main` counts first-parent commits since `v1.4.0` — `b40e2ff`, `869b43f` and the merge of `test` — so production reports `1.4.3.058`. `1.4.2` never reached production: CI failed on `869b43f` (format) and on `9c421fc` (lint), and a red CI never deploys.

## Database

- **Migration 057** — `bot_messages.is_achievement`, backfilled from `publications` plus a digest album's uncaptioned sibling photos (same `sent_at`, within ten ids). Rehearsed on a copy of production: only `schema_migrations` changed in row count; 111 published messages and 27 album photos marked, no achievement post left unmarked.
- **Migration 058** — `tracked_messages` rebuilt so its `kind` CHECK allows `summary_day` / `summary_month`. The two summary commands track under those kinds; the CHECK only knew `summary`, so `set_tracked_message` raised `IntegrityError` right after the report was sent (12 times on production in three days) and the next call could not replace it. `summary` stays for rows the removed `/summary` left. Rehearsed together with 057 on a fresh copy of production: row counts unchanged.
- `PRAGMA busy_timeout = 30000` on connect: concurrent writers wait instead of raising `database is locked`.

## Close button

- `views/keyboards.py::close_button` / `with_close_button`, callback `msg:close`. One handler (`msg_close`, which also answers the old `who:cancel` and the SPA's `ui:close`) deletes the message and drops its `online_auto_refresh` row when it was `/online`.

## Badges and names

- `AchievementBadge.COMPLETED_XBOX` (🌀) and `COMPLETED_STEAM` (👾); `views/parts.py` exposes `COMPLETED_BADGE_XBOX` / `_STEAM` / `_PSN`. The SPA (`Person.tsx`, `ui.tsx`) uses the same glyphs.
- `platform_header_lines` takes `gamertag_modern` and names the account through `xbox_nickname()` — the one Xbox chain — instead of `gamertag or "no gamertag"`.

## Sync

- `title_history` upserts keep a title on `xbox_360` once it is known to be one, and move that title's `seen_achievements` rows from `xbox_modern` to `xbox_360` (duplicates dropped). `TitleCatalogService` realigns a title whose catalog reply reports a different platform (`repo.update_title_platform`).
- `scripts/pull_games_and_achievements.py` refreshes the deep Xbox title history first and logs catalog progress with an ETA.

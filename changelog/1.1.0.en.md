# 1.1.0 — 17 September 2026

A month of work in one update. The headline: the bot speaks English now, it
stopped losing PlayStation DLC trophies, and it no longer confuses whose
achievements are whose when somebody changes account.

## New

**Two languages.** The bot speaks Russian and English. A group picks its own
language; a person picks their own for direct messages. Everything follows:
commands, cards, summaries, achievement names, game titles. Names come from
the platform itself in the language you asked for — nothing here is machine
translated.

**PlayStation DLC trophies.** The bot had never once fetched one. A game's
trophy list is split into groups — the base game plus one per DLC — and the
bot only ever asked for the base. Finishing a DLC could only make your
counter look worse. They are published and counted now, and the card names
the group with your progress through it.

**A progress counter on every notification.** "5/55" beside the game name:
how many of its achievements you have. Works on Xbox One/Series and on
PlayStation; before this only Xbox 360 had it.

**A secret achievement says so.** The header now reads "gets a secret
achievement", while the name and description stay behind a spoiler. A blurred
word with nothing explaining it read as a rendering glitch rather than as a
deliberate secret.

**Changing account works properly.** Achievement history belongs to the
account, not to the person: unlink it and the history waits, link it back and
it returns. If somebody else takes over an account, the bot asks first and
tells the previous owner. Relinking no longer dumps your whole history into
the chat.

**All three platforms, everywhere.** Xbox, PlayStation and Steam are equals in
`/panel`, `/subscribe`, `/stats` and every list. Several screens used to
require Xbox silently: a Steam- or PlayStation-only member got a stripped
panel and could not subscribe at all.

**Rarity for older achievements.** The bot only learns how rare an
achievement is during an ordinary poll of the game; everything that arrived
in the one-off import at connect time had none. Now the whole history has it
— on the live bot, known rare achievements went from 479 to 7 122.

**The PlayStation profile link points somewhere that exists.** Sony shut the
old address down back in 2021.

## Commands and cards

`/summary` is gone. **`/summary_day`** and **`/summary_month`** replace it,
both listed in the command menu. `/summary` sent both reports in one message
and was the longest thing the bot produced.

Both reports share one shape now: **Total**, then **Players**, then **Games**.
The daily and the monthly differ only in the window they cover.

On a player's card the counters name their period honestly: **"Last 24h"**
(rolling) and **"Since September 1"** (calendar month). Beside the count you
now see how many were rare, and PlayStation trophies broken down by tier.

A platinum trophy and a 100%-completed game got their own badge, **💠**. They
used to share the cup with the most ordinary achievement there is.

In `/recent`, a PlayStation row leads with its trophy tier, and the game and
the achievement are separated by a dot.

## Fixed

- **Commands answered with nothing.** On a real chat, `/summary` and `/stats`
  hit Telegram's message-length limit — the command looked dead and the
  reason was visible only in the log. An oversized message is now trimmed
  rather than lost.
- **Statistics counted imported history as earned today.** One member showed
  767 achievements for a month in which they had earned none.
- **`/recent` showed the player's lifetime gamerscore** instead of what the
  achievement was worth — the same number on every row.
- **Steam games rendered as "no title".**
- **The admin card's reset and refresh buttons raised an error on every
  press.**
- **A reconnected PlayStation account stopped being polled.**
- An achievement with no usable unlock date (Microsoft sends a placeholder
  for some) no longer disappears from statistics.

## For the administrator

The bot reports its version as the last line of `/help`: `A.B.C.D` —
architecture, line of work, build number, database schema. One line answers
"am I looking at the test bot or the real one".

**A database newer than the code refuses to start.** That is the 14 September
incident in one check: a script run from a working branch applied that
branch's migrations to the production database, and the production bot
crashed on tables it had never heard of.

The number of rows in `/recent` is now a setting on the limits screen.

An alert about a shared key going dead (Steam, PlayStation, Anthropic) is
sent only after several consecutive failed checks, and recovery is announced
separately. A single hiccup used to cost a night's alarm over a credential
that was working a minute later.

## Under the hood

Every screen's layout moved into its own layer, and mockups are rendered on
demand instead of being kept as a separate document that drifts from what the
bot actually sends. Lists share one shape: the games list in `/stats` and in
both summaries is one template with a different window and a different set of
players.

The full list of changes is in the commit history and the repository's closed
issues.

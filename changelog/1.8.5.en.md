# 1.8.5 — hotfix — 12 October 2026

[Русский](1.8.5.ru.md) · **English**

A hotfix: trophy pictures, and a Steam history behind a hidden profile.

## Posts

- **White trophies on a transparent ground show again.** Some games' trophy icons (Aniimo on PlayStation, for one) are a white drawing with no ground. Telegram filled the transparency with white, and the post showed an empty square. Such a picture is now set on a card with a ground.

## Accounts

- **A Steam history arrives once the profile is opened.** If game details were hidden when Steam was connected, the bot took it for a broken key, loaded nothing in silence and never tried again. Now it says the details are hidden, and once they are opened it loads the whole history by itself, posting nothing to the chats. Whoever lost their history this way before gets it after the update.

## For the super-admin

- **API load in more detail.** The admin home shows requests over 5 minutes, an hour and a day: Xbox per account (the median and the busiest), Steam, PSN, Anthropic with its tokens; sign-in emails name their mail server. Counted since the bot last started.

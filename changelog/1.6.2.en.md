# 1.6.2 — 1 October 2026

A new personal panel: a posting switch for each account, up to three PSN accounts, a live sync status and buttons instead of commands. A notification leads straight to the game's page, Steam's secret achievements get descriptions, and description translations are more reliable.

## Panel in DMs

- **One row per platform.** "🟢 XBOX ▸" opens that platform's screen (profile, posting, unlink), with a 🔔/🔇 switch beside it. A platform not connected is one "🎮 Connect" button.
- **Posting is per account.** One account's announcements can be switched off without unlinking it: its achievements still count in stats, summaries and `/online`, they are just not posted anywhere.
- **Up to three PSN accounts.** One person can link several PlayStation accounts, each with its own switch.
- **"📣 Post: All / Rare / None" — one mode for every chat.** One button in the panel picks it (each tap moves to the next), not a setting in each chat. "Rare" follows each chat's rarity threshold. The digest size became a chat setting.
- **Login status.** One row per platform, status only: 🔘 not connected, ✅, ⚠️, ❓. A Steam or PSN button carries ❗ while the account's achievements are hidden by its privacy settings.
- **A dead XBOX login.** "🔄 Connect again" takes the switch's place in the XBOX row and comes first on the XBOX screen.
- **Buttons instead of commands.** Unlinking, signing in again, turning off reminders, linking Steam and picking a timezone no longer ask you to type a command: it all happens in the same message, with "‹ Back", "‹ To the panel" or "⚙️ Panel".
- **The DM command menu** is `/panel`, `/stats`, `/hltb`, `/help`. `/help` in a DM says briefly what the bot is and opens the app.

## Connecting accounts

- **A live sync status.** While the bot reads an account's history, one message shows the progress — "40 of 120 games · 312 trophies found" with a bar — and updates every few seconds. It ends as the result: synced, some games private, achievements hidden, or an error — then with a "🔄 Try again" button.
- **PSN syncs faster** — about one and a half times.
- **Fuller Xbox 360 history.** Games Xbox dropped from its general list are found again, and old games completed to 100% count as completed.
- **Linking again after a deletion.** A deleted account can be linked again right away; the one-day limit only kicks in after repeated deletions. Each PSN account keeps its own count, and PSN allows as many free re-links as accounts were linked.

## Notifications and descriptions

- **The button leads to the game.** "Open Mini App" under an achievement or trophy notification (and under a single-game digest) opens that game's page — on the progress of the person who earned it.
- **Steam secret achievement descriptions.** Steam's API does not give them even once earned — they now come from the profile page, in Russian and English. Older achievements are filled in gradually.
- **Description translations.** English text and placeholder strings are no longer passed off as Russian: only Cyrillic text counts as Russian. Everything that slipped through untranslated is translated again.

## Groups

- **Scheduled day and month reports** no longer carry a "Close" button, so nobody removes them for the whole chat by accident. Reports asked for by command keep it.
- **A "⚙️ Settings" button** under the `/subscribe` reply and under `/help` leads to the DM — instead of a `/panel` command that fired in the group itself.
- **Profile links** on cards are now one super-admin setting for everybody (on by default), not each person's own.
- The club command is `/promo` only.

## Mini App

- **The "About" tab.** A table: genre, release date (with regional dates when they differ), publisher, developer, modes and score. Its last row is the time to beat — main, extra and completionist; tap it to unfold every figure, co-op and multiplayer included. The game's description sits under the table.
- **The game header.** The cover folds into a thin strip with a swipe up and back with a swipe down; a game completed to 100% keeps a compact platinum plate. A horizontal swipe between tabs no longer moves the cover.
- **Achievement tips.** An achievement with a book in its corner unfolds into a tip on how to get it, from the Steam community's most popular guides. The text is taken from the guide as it is — with links, pictures, videos, lists and tables (a wide table scrolls sideways); greetings, thanks and a retelling of the description are left out. A game has tips when its guide is split by the achievements' names; PlayStation's platinum has none. A secret one is revealed first, as before. One tip is open at a time in a group; switching tabs folds everything that was open, and closing a card brings its top back into view.
- **"Updates" tab.** A game's latest patches from its developer's announcements on Steam, grouped by day, opening in place, with links, videos and tables working. Xbox and PlayStation games show their Steam version's patches; the tab is hidden when there are none. Patches are never sent to a chat.
- **Game tabs.** A looping tab row with the active one first; the swipe between tabs is a little less sensitive and does nothing inside an open tip; the top spacing is the same on every tab.
- **An empty month.** A tidy square block, "Quiet this month so far", with a "See last month" button replaces the grey line — on the home page, in the feed and in another person's profile; the games block is not shown when there are no games.
- **Accounts in settings** are back to icons (profile, sync, unlink) in one row; the app has no "Posting" button — the switch stays in the bot. The bright stretch of a progress bar for the month now fades in, and the "Day / Month" filter on statistics is styled again.
- **Profile and games.** Games sort by date or by completion percentage, and headers carry counts. The month is picked once for the feed, the summary and the profile; pull-to-refresh is off on past months.
- **Speed.** The admin panel, settings, the connect form and the game page load only when needed, so the home screen opens faster.
- **Look.** Softer darkening on feed covers, a larger name in the profile header, a cleaner PlayStation logo.

## Data

- **HowLongToBeat.** More is now kept from a game's page: score, developer and publisher, release dates, modes, and co-op and multiplayer times. Figures that describe the site's audience (how many finished, are playing, shelved a game) are deliberately not kept. A game already in the cache has its page re-read once on the next lookup.
